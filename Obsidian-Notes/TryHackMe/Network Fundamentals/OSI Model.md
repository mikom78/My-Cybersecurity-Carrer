* There are 7 layers at all.


###### **7. Application (The Interface)**

- The Action: You decide to send a gift. You open the "Delivery App" on your phone.
- Data Form: This is where the user interacts with the data.
###### **6. Presentation (The Preparation)**

- The Action: You wrap the watch in a box and gift wrap. You also translate the "Thank You" note into your friend's language.
- Role: This layer handles encryption and formatting (making sure the data is readable).
###### **5. Session (The Conversation)**

- The Action: You call your friend and ask, "Are you home? Can I send the package now?" You keep the line open until the delivery is confirmed.
- Role: It manages the connection between the two devices.
###### **4. Transport (The Quality Control)**

- The Action: You decide if you need a "Tracking Number" (TCP) or if it’s okay to just drop it in the mail and hope it arrives (UDP).
- Role: This is where segmentation happens. It breaks the data into pieces and numbers them so they can be rebuilt.
###### **3. Network (The GPS)**

- The Action: You write the IP Address (the street address) on the box. The delivery truck looks at the map to find the fastest route.
- Role: Handles Routing and IP Addressing.
###### **2. Data Link (The Local Delivery)**

- The Action: The delivery truck arrives at the specific apartment complex and looks for the MAC Address (the physical door number).
- Role: Handles the Physical Address and error checking on the local cable.
###### **1. Physical (The Road)**

- The Action: The actual truck driving on the physical asphalt road.
- Role: This is the hardware—the cables, the electrical pulses, or the Wi-Fi radio waves.
